Data granularity

Two data levels are included:

  • Summary level：from manually curated sheets (2026-10-21), with Top1–3 candidates, sequences, scores and run notes;
  • Full-package level：from each run's tar.gz，including all candidates, AF2 metrics, tail-fiber candidates, pvc13 reprogrammed sequences and nanomachine components.

The data level is shown as a badge; new runs are stored preferentially as full packages.
